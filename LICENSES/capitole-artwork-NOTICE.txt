Our changes: export the original KRA with Krita 5.3.4; resize the original
layers and offsets from a 3340 x 3340 canvas to 1000 x 1000; normalize to
8-bit sRGB RGBA; translate layer names into Chinese. Preserve the original
7 painting layers, group, visibility, blend operations and opacity. Rebuild
the merged preview and thumbnail with MoonOpenRaster. No new painted or
text layers are added. This conversion is reproducible with
scripts/prepare_artwork.py. Original-to-translated names and the independent
Krita comparison are recorded in fixtures/artwork-validation.json.

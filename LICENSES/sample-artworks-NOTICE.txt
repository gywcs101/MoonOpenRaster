No generative image models were used to invent, split, repaint or complete
these original artwork layers. No endorsement by the artist is implied.

Original-mode conversions: export the author's KRA with Krita 5.3.4, convert
to 8-bit sRGB raster OpenRaster, resize layers/offsets proportionally to a
maximum canvas side of 768 pixels, translate layer names, and regenerate
previews and thumbnails with MoonOpenRaster. All PNG layers in these samples
come from Krita's export of the original source artwork. Rain City's original
two adjustment-layer effects are baked by Krita into the exported raster
result; it has nine editable raster layers, not full KRA filter semantics.
The KRA versus ORA stored previews and independently rendered web-sized
variants are checked; see fixtures/sample-validation.json.

Practice-mode modifications are deliberate and separately labelled: change
visibility, opacity or offsets of existing layers; In Bloom's reference
result exposes its original grayscale underpainting; Rain City's reference
result reduces its rain layer. House restores the original visual arrangement.
The Capitole exercise uses the existing artwork credited separately in
capitole-artwork-NOTICE.txt, with a flat green tint applied to the original
white background as an exercise defect. Its replacement PNG is the original
white background layer. These practice variants are not presented as the
unaltered originals or as the author's preferred revisions.

Covered files: web/assets/samples/* artwork, the artwork thumbnails/reference
images in fixtures/samples/, and embedded raster artwork within their ORA
archives. These retain the source terms and are not relicensed under MIT.
Exercise instructions, sample metadata and processing scripts are project code.

Reproduction: download the three pinned zips into ../.tools/artwork/, build
the JS core, then run scripts/prepare_samples.py with the Krita executable.
The existing Capitole source sample is prepared by scripts/prepare_artwork.py.
scripts/build_sample_catalog.mjs creates the practice variants by replaying
real MoonBit document edits; it does not flatten the file into a mock editor.
